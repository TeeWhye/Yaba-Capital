Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need collateral to apply?

Most Daily and Weekly loans are unsecured and based on your business's trading history. Larger Expansion Loans may require security depending on the amount requested.

How fast will I receive funds?

Most applicants receive a decision within one working day, and funds are disbursed shortly after acceptance — typically within 24 hours of approval.

What documents do I need?

A valid ID, proof of business (such as a shop address or trading history), and a bank account or wallet to receive funds. Additional documents may be requested for larger loans.

What happens if I repay early?

Early repayment is encouraged — a clean repayment history increases the amount you're eligible for on your next loan.
